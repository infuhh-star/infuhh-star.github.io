STAFFWORKS CRM CALENDAR

Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal calendar connection used by an owner-managed Twenty CRM workspace.

Information accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the connection accesses Google calendar events and basic account identity, including the account's name and email address. Calendar data can include event titles, descriptions, dates, participants, locations, meeting links and recurrence information. The Google authorization screen states the permissions requested.

The configured permission allows access to calendar events; it does not grant Gmail access. The current synchronization imports events from the connected account's primary calendar.

Use and storage

Google data is used to synchronize calendar activity into the owner's self-hosted Twenty CRM for scheduling and viewing that activity. Connection credentials and imported calendar records are held within the owner's CRM deployment. Calendar visibility is controlled in Twenty; the current workspace shares only event dates and participants with other workspace users. Automatic contact creation from event participants is disabled.

The integration's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data accessed by this integration is not sold or used for advertising or to train general-purpose AI models.

Public website and service providers

This public website does not receive Google calendar data or authorization tokens. It contains no sign-in form, advertising or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process standard website-request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

The CRM communicates with Google to perform synchronization. Access by optional CRM features is controlled by the workspace's settings and the owner's actions.

Retention and deletion

The workspace owner controls retention of imported records and local backups. Disconnect the account in Twenty to stop synchronization, and revoke access in Google Account connections to withdraw Google's authorization. Disconnecting or revoking access may leave previously imported CRM records and backups; the owner can remove those through workspace administration and backup management.

Questions or changes

Contact the workspace owner about access, deletion or this policy. This page will be updated when the integration's data practices change.
