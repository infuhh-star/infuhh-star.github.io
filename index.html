PERSONAL WORKSPACE INTEGRATION

Staffworks CRM Calendar

Calendar synchronization for an owner-managed Twenty CRM workspace.

What the connection does

The workspace owner connects their Google Calendar through Google's authorization screen. The local Twenty deployment imports calendar events for scheduling and viewing calendar activity within the CRM.

The connection requests calendar-event access and basic Google account information so the workspace can identify the connected account. Gmail access is not requested. Automatic creation of contacts from calendar attendees is disabled in this installation.

Where the application runs

The CRM runs in the owner's self-hosted environment. This website provides information about that connection. It has no Google sign-in flow and does not receive calendar events or Google connection credentials.

Account control

The owner can pause calendar synchronization or disconnect the account in Twenty's account settings. Google access can also be revoked in the Google Account's third-party connections settings.

For questions about this personal integration, contact the workspace owner.
