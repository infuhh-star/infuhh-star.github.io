Terms of use

Information for the owner and authorized users of this personal calendar connection.

Personal workspace use

This integration connects a Google account to an owner-managed Twenty CRM workspace. It is intended for the workspace owner's personal operation and users the owner authorizes. Connect only an account and calendar that you own or are authorized to use.

Permissions and availability

Connecting a Google account requires authorization through Google. You can pause synchronization or disconnect the account in Twenty and revoke access in your Google Account. Google and Twenty settings, API availability and the local CRM's operation can affect synchronization. Check the source calendar when confirming an appointment.

Data practices

The privacy policy describes data accessed and stored by the connection. This information website does not provide access to the CRM or calendar records. Google and Twenty remain subject to their respective licenses and applicable terms.
